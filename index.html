Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

Earnings vary based on hours driven, location, and demand. Full-time drivers in Kathmandu typically earn NPR 60,000-100,000+ per month. Part-time drivers can earn NPR 20,000-40,000. You also receive weekly bonuses for completing trip targets and keep 100% of your tips.

Your vehicle must be a 4-door car in good condition, less than 10 years old, with valid registration and insurance. Common approved models include Maruti Swift, Hyundai i10, Toyota Etios, and similar vehicles. The vehicle must pass our inspection before you can start driving.

You receive weekly direct deposits to your bank account every Monday for trips completed the previous week. There are no hidden fees, and you can track all your earnings in real-time through the Yango Pro app. Tips are paid out instantly.

Absolutely! Many of our drivers work part-time around their other commitments. You have complete flexibility to choose when and how many hours you want to drive. There's no minimum hour requirement. Drive during peak hours for maximum earnings.

You get 24/7 support through phone, in-app chat, and email. Our dedicated driver support team helps with any issues, from technical problems to payment questions. We also provide training materials, safety resources, and regular updates on how to maximize your earnings.

Once you submit your complete application with all required documents, approval typically takes 24-48 hours. After approval, you'll receive instructions to download the Yango Pro app, complete a brief orientation, and you can start accepting rides immediately.

While most drivers use their own vehicles, we also work with vehicle rental partners. If you don't own a suitable vehicle, we can connect you with rental options. However, owning your vehicle typically results in higher net earnings.

Yango takes a transparent service fee from each ride, and you keep the rest. The exact percentage varies by location but is always clearly shown before you accept a ride. There are no hidden charges, and during promotional periods, commission rates may be reduced further.

About Yango

Yango is a global ride-hailing service from the Yango Group that connects passengers with professional drivers through advanced technology. Operating in 25 countries across the Middle East, Africa, Latin America, and Asia, Yango has established itself as a trusted transportation platform that prioritizes both driver success and passenger satisfaction.

Using cutting-edge mapping, navigation systems, and smart order distribution algorithms, Yango delivers a cost-effective, convenient experience for passengers while optimizing operational efficiency and earnings for partner drivers.

In Nepal, Yango has quickly become one of the fastest-growing ride-hailing platforms, helping thousands of drivers achieve financial independence and flexibility in their work lives.

About Drivra Mobility

Drivra Mobility is a leading transportation solutions partner in Nepal, committed to revolutionizing how people move and earn in the mobility sector. As an official partner of Yango, we facilitate driver recruitment, provide local support, and ensure seamless operations across Nepal.

Our mission is to empower individuals with flexible earning opportunities while contributing to better transportation infrastructure in Nepal. We understand the local market, driver needs, and passenger expectations, making us the ideal bridge between global technology and local execution.

Beyond driver recruitment, Drivra Mobility is innovating mobility solutions to enhance urban transportation in Nepal, always with a focus on sustainability, efficiency, and community impact.
